# Weirdest Job Title: Blind Judge Showdown

A one-title entry for the make.fun competition **Weirdest Job Title:
Blind Judge Showdown**: a single job title of at most 60 characters,
judged head-to-head in blind rounds on which title is weirder and
funnier -- a real-sounding job with one hilariously specific twist
beats a generic oddity.

## The title

> Professional Mourner for Strangers, Paid Per Convincing Sob

The professional mourner is a real odd job; this one works only for
strangers and is paid per convincing sob -- a gig-economy rate card
you can picture mid-sob at a graveside, invoicing by the tear.

## Run

`main.py` (Python 3) deterministically writes the title to
`/output/entry.txt` (override the directory with
`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` for local runs). No inputs, no network.
